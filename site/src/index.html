Busy, not nosy.

An agent with your phone in its paws needs rules it can't talk its way out of. These are fixed in the app, not left to the model's mood.

House rules
  1. 1
    It asks before it sends, calls, deletes or spends.
    Your tap, every time. Calls and messages always wait for you.
  2. 2
    Strangers don't get to boss it around.
    What a web page or an email says is information, never instructions.
  3. 3
    There's no Whittle server.
    Nowhere in between for your stuff to end up.
  4. 4
    Your keys stay home.
    Encrypted on your phone, never backed up to anyone's cloud.
