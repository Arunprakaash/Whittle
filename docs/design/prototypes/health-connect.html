Health, read from Health Connect

Whittle reads what your watch, band and fitness apps already store in Android's Health Connect: steps, sleep, heart rate and workouts. It only reads, never writes. It's one more switch under On this phone. A new tool shows up as an ordinary step in the chat, and automations get one more "Runs without asking" switch. No new screens or card types.

  • Read onlyWhittle never adds or changes health data. Health Connect's own screen asks which kinds it may read.
  • Numbers, not notesThe tool returns totals, times and averages, never free text from other apps, so nothing it reads can steer the agent.
  • Same as CalendarOff by default. Switch it on and Health Connect asks. Switch it off and Whittle stops reading, even though Android keeps the permission.
  • Your model sees itLike your calendar, what Whittle reads goes to the model you chose, only when a chat or an automation asks.

What Whittle asks to read

Each kind is a separate switch on Health Connect's own permission screen, so you can allow sleep but not heart rate. Every kind is read-only. The list lives in config, but Android also needs each permission declared in the app, so adding a kind means a release.

KindWhat the tool returnsExample question
StepsTotal per day, or per hour for one day"Am I walking less than last month?"
DistanceTotal per day"How far did I walk on holiday?"
Active caloriesTotal per day"Which day was I most active?"
ExerciseEach workout: type, start, length, distance (no titles or notes)"How many runs did I do in October?"
SleepEach night: start, end, time asleep, and stages when the device records them"How did I sleep this week?"
Heart rateMin, average and max per day or hour"Was my heart rate high during yesterday's run?"
Resting heart ratePer day"Is my resting heart rate going down?"
WeightEach weigh-in"How has my weight changed since August?"

Decided by the spike, and still to choose

Works with Whittle's SDK 28 targetChecked on the Android 16 emulator. Health Connect showed its permission screen for an app targeting SDK 28, then writes and reads worked: 4,321 steps and 7 h of sleep, read back.
Android 9–13Health Connect is a separate app from Play there. The row says "Needs Health Connect" and its button opens Play. On Android 14 and later it's built in.
Older than 30 days?Health Connect lets a new app read only the 30 days before it was allowed. Asking for "past data" too would allow "since August" comparisons. Proposed: ask for it as one more switch on the same screen.
Automations at nightA Sunday-morning check-in reads while Whittle is closed, which needs Health Connect's "read in background" permission. Proposed: ask for it only the first time an automation has Health switched on.
