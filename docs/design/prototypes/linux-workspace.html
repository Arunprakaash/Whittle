What changes

A picture in the reference's patternPale ground, far shapes in two close greys striped like dithered pixels, one character in colour, a ground line. The character is Whittle's own beaver sprite, and the far shapes are pine woods. It blinks when Linux is ready; while Linux sets up it gnaws its stick, shavings fly, and the ground line fills as the progress bar.
Your computers firstLike the reference's Devices: each computer you added is a chip, + adds one, and a green dot marks one that's signed in now. Tap a chip to open that computer's page: whether it's signed in now, its key's type and fingerprint, and Remove this computer, confirmed in a dialog.
The switch moves nothingThe Wi-Fi and Tailscale chips sit on one row that scrolls, and the sign-in command stays in place. Off, they dim. The command has no note: the Wi-Fi chip above it already says where it works.
Running, only while something runsA green dot, the name and its port; grey and "Starting…" until something answers on the port. Stop all sits on the label. The command and the running time are left out.
