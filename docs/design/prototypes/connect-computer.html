Connect to your phone, simpler

The Mac app's first screen, cut down to what you do on it: type the phone's address and connect. The beaver scene says how the link stands, so it's the heading too. Setting up a phone for the first time is two short rows under Connect, and they fold away once a phone has connected. Try the states, and type in the field.

What changes

One thing to doThe address and Connect are the screen. The two-column layout, the intro paragraph and the lock note go; the scene's title says what this is.
Setup only when it's neededTwo rows of a few words each: turn it on, add this Mac's key. After the first connection they fold into "Set up another phone", and the address is filled in.
The key, one tapThe key isn't shown in full. A copy button copies it, and the row shows its type and its last few characters so you can tell it's this Mac's.
Problems where they happenThe scene's status turns red, and the line under the address says what to do. "Not added" opens the setup rows.
