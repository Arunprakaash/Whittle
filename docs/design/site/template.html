Whittle Design System

Every visual and interaction decision for the Android app, in one place. This page is generated from the token file, so what you see here is exactly what Figma and the Compose theme get.

Color

Seed Pine with a Marigold accent. Tonal Material 3 roles: surfaces separate content by tone, not borders. Marigold only marks a choice for you: decision cards and open-card badges. Wallpaper colors (Material You) is an opt-in setting.

Creation tones

Each thing a user makes gets the same rounded-square tile and one of six tones, picked from its id. Icons on the shape are at least 4.5:1 in both themes.

Typography

Clean and friendly. One family, Figtree, in three weights (400, 500, 600): open, round letters that stay easy to read for people who never use developer tools. Geist Mono for step details and timers. Both bundled. No width stretching, no black weights, at most one emphasized style per screen.

Shape & elevation

Plain rounded rectangles only, no decorative shapes. Segmented groups: rows 2dp apart, outer corners lIncreased, inner corners s. In button groups the selected item morphs to fully rounded.

Iconography

Material Symbols Rounded, weight 400, grade 0, optical size 24 (20 below 24dp). Outline by default; filled for selected states and inside creation tiles. No sparkles, wands or robots: Whittle's mark is the agent dot.

Motion

Material 3 Expressive springs. Spatial springs move, resize and reshape and may overshoot; effects springs change color and opacity and never do. Click a card to play it.

Haptics

One HapticsController maps these events. It respects the system touch-feedback setting and the in-app switch, and never fires in the background or per streamed word. Tap "Feel it" on an Android phone.

Components

Decisions and questions never block or pop up: they are inline cards, the agent keeps working, and a tap continues the run from that point. Rendered from the tokens in both app themes. Each will live in ui/designsystem/components with previews for light, dark, 200% font and reduced motion.

Accessibility, do and don't

Accessibility

  • 48dp touch targets; text reflows at 200% font scale; no fixed-height text containers.
  • TalkBack hears one "Working: checking your calendar" per step, never per token; the answer is read once.
  • Decision cards are announced politely once ("Needs your choice: Send this to Priya?"); nothing else is announced and nothing takes focus.
  • Every icon-only button has a content description.
  • State is never shown by color, motion or haptics alone.
  • Key-entry screens use FLAG_SECURE.

Do and don't

check_circleLead with what the user has and what's happening.
cancel"How can I help you today?" empty states.
check_circleOrganise with tone and segmented groups.
cancelBorders and shadows on everything; gradients, glows, sparkles.
check_circleName actions precisely; keep tool details one tap away.
cancelGeneric "Allow?" / "OK"; JSON or model names in the thread.
check_circleHaptics at milestones; real results only.
cancelVibrating per word; fake progress or unbacked success.
